## Direction contract

seed: 645a83dc (assigned 4 of 7: gymnasium court floor)

THESIS: The app is a painted sports court. Time is drawn as court lines in the period's own paint colour, not as a widget. Refuses the category default: a tomato-red circle on a white card, or neon digits on black.

OWN-WORLD: Light = sealed maple floor (warm honey ground, deeper grain-tone panels), dark = night court (deep blue-black poured floor). Two paint colours carry every state: court blue = WORK, key orange = EXERCISE; white boundary line paint for neutral rules. Condensed athletic signage face (Barlow Condensed) for numerals and headings, Barlow for UI text. Tabular figures. Lines are flat painted strokes with square ends; no glass, no gradients.

STORY: Glance at the window: one colour tells you work or exercise, one giant number tells you how long, the exercise name tells you what to do.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Timer screen. Centre circle of the court is the radial timer; the remaining arc is a thick painted line in the period colour over a faint white boundary circle. Huge condensed digits in the middle, exercise name under. Controls are painted-line buttons along the bottom. Mini: the same line as a horizontal lane stripe.

SIGNATURE: At a boundary the whole floor "repaints": the period colour floods in from the ring outward (one authored transition), then settles. Raise from labanotation (declined): in Setup, each sequence step's bar length is proportional to its duration, so the sequence reads as a time score. Raise from gate board (declined): the "waiting for Start work" state stays lit in the exercise colour until acknowledged.

RISK: Maple ground could drift toward cream; keep it a saturated honey wood tone, not paper.
